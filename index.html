About the App

Noon Islamic is your comprehensive Islamic companion, designed to help you stay connected with your faith throughout the day. Whether you're at home or on the go, access essential Islamic resources anytime, anywhere.

  • Quran with Tafsir
  • Prayer Times
  • Qibla Direction
  • Azkar and Supplications
  • Islamic Reminders
  • Quran Radio
